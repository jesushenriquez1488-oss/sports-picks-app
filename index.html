STRAIGHT ANSWERS
Is this just ChatGPT with a skin on it?
No. Each sport runs its own statistical model with its own inputs — park factors and bullpen fatigue for baseball, schedule strength and positional injury weighting for football, rest and back-to-backs for basketball. No language model writes the projection.
Why do some days have almost no flagged games?
Because the model didn't find enough separation from the line. A day with two flagged matchups is the system working, not failing.
The lines move. Does that change anything?
Yes. Every edge is measured against the line at the time of the analysis, and that line is recorded with the result. If the market moves, the edge you'd get later isn't the one the model measured.
Do you delete the losses?
No. Every published result is graded automatically and stays on the record with the line it was recorded at.
Can I cancel?
Any time, from your account. No call, no email, no retention script.
Is this betting advice?
No. CashEdge is a statistical analysis tool. What you do with the output is your call.
