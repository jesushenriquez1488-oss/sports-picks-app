Delete Your CashEdge Account

You may request deletion of your CashEdge account and associated personal information at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send a deletion request to CashEdge Support using the email address associated with your CashEdge account.

The email should include the email address used for your CashEdge account so we can identify and verify the account.

Request Account Deletion

Tap the button below to create a pre-filled account deletion request to CashEdge Support.

REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION

What Will Be Deleted

Once the request has been verified and processed, CashEdge may delete or anonymize information associated with the account, including:

  • CashEdge account information
  • Account authentication records associated with the deleted account
  • User-specific platform information that is no longer required to operate the service
  • Personal information that CashEdge is not required to retain

Information We May Retain

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, financial, tax, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Information retained for these purposes will not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

Premium Subscriptions

If you have an active Premium subscription, please manage or cancel future renewals through Manage subscription in CashEdge, or tell CashEdge Support in your deletion request that you also need assistance with your subscription.

Account deletion and subscription billing are separate processes. Deleting an account does not automatically create a refund for previous subscription charges.

Verification

CashEdge may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing the deletion request.

This helps prevent unauthorized users from requesting deletion of another person's account.
