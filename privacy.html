CASHEDGE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

CashEdge ("CashEdge", "we", "our", or "us") provides sports analytics, statistical projections, Premium Plays, player prop analysis, market intelligence, and related sports information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, the technologies and service providers we use, and the privacy choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CashEdge, we may collect:

  • Email address used for registration and account access
  • User ID and authentication information
  • Authentication provider information when you choose services such as Google Sign-In
  • Subscription and Premium account status
  • Payment and subscription records
  • Sports, games, pages, and features viewed or used
  • Platform interaction and usage events
  • Session identifiers
  • Device, browser, IP address, operating system, and technical information
  • Referral, advertising, campaign, and conversion information when permitted
  • Advertising identifiers or click identifiers when available and permitted
  • Push notification subscription or device identifiers when notifications are enabled

Account and Authentication Data

CashEdge uses Supabase for account authentication, user records, subscription status, platform databases, and certain internal platform functions.

Users may sign in using supported authentication methods, including email/password and Google Sign-In.

When Google Sign-In is used, information necessary to authenticate the account, such as the user's email address and authentication identifier, may be provided through the authentication process.

Platform and Usage Data

When Analytics is enabled, CashEdge may record usage events such as application visits, sports viewed, pages accessed, features used, session identifiers, and related interaction information.

This information may be used to understand how CashEdge is used, troubleshoot platform issues, and improve the service.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

CashEdge uses cookies, local storage, device storage, and similar technologies to operate the platform, remember privacy preferences, maintain sessions, understand platform usage, and measure advertising performance.

These technologies are grouped into the following categories:

  • Necessary: Required for authentication, security, subscriptions, payment processing, privacy preferences, and core CashEdge functionality.
  • Analytics: Used to understand how users interact with CashEdge and help improve the platform.
  • Advertising: Used to measure advertising performance, registrations, subscriptions, and conversions through advertising platforms such as Meta, Google, and TikTok.

Optional Analytics and Advertising technologies are controlled through CashEdge's privacy preference system.

Analytics

If Analytics is enabled, CashEdge may use Google Analytics and CashEdge's internal analytics systems to understand how the platform is used.

Analytics data may include pages or features viewed, sports selected, application activity, session information, device and browser information, and similar usage data.

If Analytics is not enabled, CashEdge does not intentionally activate optional Google Analytics tracking or optional CashEdge usage-event tracking for that user.

Advertising and Conversion Measurement

If Advertising is enabled, CashEdge may use advertising measurement technologies including:

  • Meta Pixel
  • Meta Conversions API
  • Google Ads
  • TikTok Pixel
  • TikTok advertising and conversion measurement systems

Advertising measurement may include events such as:

  • Page or application visits
  • Account registrations
  • Premium subscription purchases

Depending on the advertising platform and event, information used for measurement may include browser or device information, IP address, user agent, advertising or click identifiers, event identifiers, purchase value, currency, and event source information.

Certain identifiers may be hashed before being transmitted through server-side advertising measurement systems.

If Advertising is not enabled, CashEdge does not intentionally activate optional advertising measurement technologies for that user.

Your Privacy Choices

CashEdge provides the following privacy choices:

  • Accept All
  • Reject Non-Essential
  • Manage Preferences

Analytics and Advertising preferences may be controlled separately.

Necessary technologies cannot be disabled because they are required for core platform functions.

CashEdge stores privacy choices in local storage so the platform can remember the user's selection.

Preferences can be changed later through Cookie Preferences.

Changing a privacy preference affects future optional tracking. Technologies already loaded during a prior authorized session may require the page or application to reload before they are fully removed.

Payments and Subscriptions

Payments and subscription billing are processed through Stripe.

CashEdge does not store full payment card numbers.

Stripe may process information necessary to complete payments, manage subscriptions, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Push Notifications

CashEdge may use OneSignal to provide optional push notifications.

Push notifications require permission from the user's device, browser, or operating system.

Notification services may process device or subscription identifiers necessary to deliver notifications.

Users may decline notification permission or disable notifications through their device or browser settings.

  • Provide and maintain CashEdge services
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide and manage Premium access
  • Process and maintain subscriptions
  • Provide sports analytics and platform features
  • Deliver requested notifications
  • Maintain platform security

  • Improve CashEdge and its services
