Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

CashEdge AI Sports Picks ("CashEdge", "we", "our", or "us") provides sports analytics, statistical projections, player props, premium picks, and related sports information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, the technologies and service providers we use, and the privacy choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CashEdge, we may collect:

  • Email address used for registration and account access
  • User ID and authentication information
  • Subscription and Premium account status
  • Payment and subscription records
  • Sports viewed and features used within CashEdge
  • Platform interaction and usage events
  • Session identifiers
  • Device, browser, IP address, and technical information
  • Referral, advertising, and conversion information when permitted

Account and Platform Data

CashEdge uses Supabase for account authentication, user records, subscription status, platform databases, and certain internal platform functions.

When analytics preferences are enabled, CashEdge may also record internal usage events such as application visits, sports viewed, pages or features accessed, session identifiers, and related interaction information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

CashEdge uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the platform, remember privacy preferences, maintain sessions, understand platform usage, and measure advertising performance.

We separate these technologies into the following categories:

  • Necessary: Required for account access, authentication, security, subscriptions, payment processing, privacy preferences, and core CashEdge functionality.
  • Analytics: Used to understand how users interact with CashEdge and to help improve the platform.
  • Advertising: Used to measure advertising performance, registrations, subscriptions, and conversions through advertising platforms such as Meta and Google.

Optional analytics and advertising technologies are controlled through CashEdge's privacy preference system. Users may accept all optional technologies, reject non-essential technologies, or manage analytics and advertising preferences separately.

Analytics

If a user enables Analytics, CashEdge may use Google Analytics and CashEdge's internal analytics systems to understand how the platform is used.

Analytics information may include pages or features viewed, sports selected, application activity, device and browser information, session identifiers, and similar usage information.

If Analytics is not enabled, CashEdge does not intentionally activate optional Google Analytics tracking or CashEdge's optional internal usage-event tracking for that user.

Advertising and Conversion Measurement

If a user enables Advertising, CashEdge may use Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, Google Ads, and related advertising technologies to measure advertising performance and conversions.

Advertising measurement may include events such as:

  • Page visits
  • Account registrations
  • Premium subscription purchases

For Meta advertising measurement, certain information may be transmitted through the browser or server-side systems. Depending on the event, this may include identifiers such as a hashed email address, hashed user ID, IP address, browser user agent, Meta browser identifiers such as _fbp or _fbc, event identifiers, purchase value, currency, and event source URL.

CashEdge uses hashing for certain identifiers before they are transmitted through Meta's server-side conversion system.

If Advertising is not enabled, CashEdge does not intentionally send optional advertising conversion events through Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, or Google Ads for that user.

Your Privacy Choices

Users can choose between:

  • Accept All
  • Reject Non-Essential
  • Manage Preferences

Necessary technologies cannot be disabled because they are required for core platform functions.

Analytics and Advertising preferences may be changed separately. CashEdge stores the user's privacy preference in local storage so the platform can remember the selection.

Users may change their choices at any time by selecting Cookie Preferences from the CashEdge website footer. Changes apply to future optional tracking.

Payments

Payments and subscriptions are securely processed by Stripe. CashEdge does not store full payment card numbers.

Stripe may process information necessary to complete payments, manage subscriptions, prevent fraud, and comply with its own legal obligations. Stripe's processing is subject to its own privacy practices.

Push Notifications

CashEdge may use OneSignal to provide optional push notifications. Push notifications require device or browser permission.

Users may decline notification permission or disable notifications through their device or browser settings.
